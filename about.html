About

Hi, I’m Olivia

A First Class Fashion Design graduate from Nottingham Trent University and a creative designer who loves turning ideas into something people can see, experience and connect with.

My background in fashion has given me a strong understanding of design, research, concept development and the importance of creating work with purpose and personality. I’m naturally curious and enjoy exploring ideas, experimenting with different approaches and finding new ways to communicate creatively.

I love taking an initial idea and developing it into something meaningful, whether through visual concepts, creative campaigns or thoughtful design. I bring an open mind, a fresh perspective and an enthusiasm for learning, with a genuine interest in how creativity can inspire, connect and engage people.

Outside of design, you’ll usually find me working through my bucket list, finding inspiration in unexpected places or spending time outdoors with my dogs.
